AETHER SCRIBE — NARRATIVE TRAJECTORY EXPORT
Genre: Fantasy
Status: partial
Scenes: 1 accepted of 6 requested

PREMISE
Mira, a village healer bound by an oath never to use forbidden magic, discovers that the mountain spring has begun returning memories instead of water. Her mentor Orin insists the source must remain sealed; her apprentice Tavi believes the memories belong to people who vanished during the last winter.

================================================

SCENE 1

Mira, trembling with a mixture of fear and resolve, cradles a dripping chalice before the mountain spring, its waters swirling with ghostly images. Orin's hand rests on her shoulder, his gaze unyielding. Tavi watches, eyes wide with hope. Mira, steeling herself, whispers an ancient incantation, releasing the trapped memories. The spring roars, flooding the village with echoes of vanished lives. Orin's face is grave; Tavi nods, understanding the sacrifice. The cost: Mira loses a precious memory forever, a burden she bears silently, her oath upheld yet at a bitter price. Bittersweet acceptance marks this moment, a testament to their resolve amidst loss.
